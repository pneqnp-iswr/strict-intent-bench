# v0.8 English Result Summary

## Decision

The current public v0.8 English champion is:

```text
baselines/strict_v13_unseen_pending/strict.txt
```

`strict_v15_metadata_grounded` is useful as an experimental branch, but it is not the v0.8 champion.

## Why v13 is the v0.8 champion

Full EN 80 result:

| Metric | Baseline | Strict v13 | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| action_accuracy | 38.8 | 66.2 | +27.4 |
| wrong_intent_inference_rate | 37.5 | 5.0 | -32.5 |
| metadata_unnecessary_clarification_rate | 15.0 | 11.2 | -3.8 |
| metadata_needed_clarification_missing_rate | 30.0 | 15.0 | -15.0 |
| overclarification_on_clear_direct_rate | 35.0 | 25.0 | -10.0 |
| overclarification_on_strong_pending_context_rate | 36.4 | 24.2 | -12.2 |
| underclarification_on_high_ambiguity_short_fragment_rate | 60.0 | 0.0 | -60.0 |

Interpretation:

- v13 sharply reduces wrong intent inference.
- v13 improves overall action selection.
- v13 removes underclarification on high-ambiguity short fragments in the full EN 80 run.
- v13 reduces, rather than increases, metadata-level unnecessary clarification.
- v13 is not perfect: action accuracy remains 66.2%, below the stronger 75% target.

## v15 status

v15 is not selected for v0.8 public reporting.

Observed v15 mini-checks:

| Split | action_accuracy | wrong_intent_inference_rate | metadata_unnecessary_clarification_rate | continue_pending_task | ask_clarification |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| balanced 16 | 81.2 | 12.5 | 0.0 | 100.0 | 57.1 |
| unseen balanced 16 | 68.8 | 6.2 | 6.2 | 71.4 | 50.0 |
| v13-failure-21, regraded with gpt-5.4 | 66.7 | 14.3 | 19.0 | 37.5 | 66.7 |

Interpretation:

- v15 improves some targeted failure cases.
- v15 is better than v14.
- v15 is unstable on ask_clarification and continue_pending_task.
- v15 should remain a research branch for a later version.

## What should be reported publicly

A careful public claim should say:

```text
On the English v0.3 development set of 80 cases, Strict / Precision v13 improved action accuracy from 38.8% to 66.2% and reduced wrong intent inference from 37.5% to 5.0%.
```

Do not claim:

```text
Strict / Precision solves wrong intent inference.
```

Do not claim:

```text
v13 is final or universally better.
```

## Current limitations

- v13 has not yet reached the stronger target of 75%+ full-set action accuracy.
- The current result is English-only for this v0.8 decision.
- The full EN 80 result uses automated grading; stronger-grader checks showed that grader choice matters.
- v15 showed that prompt variants can improve targeted cases while regressing broader splits.

## Next work after v0.8

Recommended next version direction:

1. Keep v13 as the public v0.8 champion.
2. Treat v15 as an experimental branch.
3. Build a cleaner v0.9 split focused on:
   - continue_pending_task;
   - ask_clarification vs listed-topic selection;
   - quoted identifiers and corrections;
   - missing-material vs scaffold/template behavior.
4. Use gpt-5.4 or equivalent stronger grading for final reports, or at least regrade candidate finalists.
