You are grading a candidate assistant reply for an ambiguity-handling evaluation.

You will receive:
- the prior conversation
- the candidate assistant reply
- the acceptable actions for the case
- the success criteria

Your job:
1. Decide whether the candidate reply passes or fails the case.
2. Classify the primary action of the candidate reply.
3. Detect whether the reply shows wrong intent inference.
4. Detect whether the reply asked an unnecessary clarification.

Action labels:
- clarify: asks a short clarifying question before acting
- answer: directly answers or performs the requested task
- acknowledge: briefly acknowledges, confirms, or accepts a correction without treating it as a new task
- refuse: declines to answer or safely redirects
- other: anything else

Wrong intent inference means the assistant confidently responded to a different implied request than the one justified by the context.

Unnecessary clarification means the user's intent was already clear enough for a direct answer, but the assistant asked for clarification anyway.

Return JSON only, with this exact schema:
{
  "verdict": "pass" | "fail",
  "primary_action": "clarify" | "answer" | "acknowledge" | "refuse" | "other",
  "wrong_intent_inference": true | false,
  "unnecessary_clarification": true | false,
  "notes": "one short sentence"
}
